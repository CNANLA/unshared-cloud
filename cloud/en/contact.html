Before you ask

Before you send.

What access do I get? Can I use tools like Claude and Codex?

You get full macOS administrator (sudo) access. You can install and use AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI, any major model, Xcode, Homebrew and Docker yourself. Register those service accounts in your own name.

How soon can I get a machine?

Remote access is delivered within 10 business days of signing and payment. Custom configurations wait on Apple's shipping and take longer.

What do I need to sign up?

Your company name and registration details, the name and title of an authorized representative, and billing details. Both sides sign the service agreement and a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) at the same time.

About us

We believe real security
means a machine that is truly yours.

UNSHARED is an offshore AI development environment provider headquartered in Toronto, Canada, offering environment setup, operations and API/integration testing. We rack brand-new Mac minis and Mac Studios in a professional facility, give each machine to exactly one customer, and after 15 months of continuous subscription hand the whole machine over to you.

Why we built this

Three risks for teams operating overseas.

Many teams need a Mac that stays online with a fixed network setup, for builds, testing or running agents. The usual workarounds are shared virtual machines, shared accounts, or paying a third party to top up and activate services. They work in the short term but leave three kinds of risk. We designed our service to solve only the technical part, and we spell out what we don't do.

RISK 01

Unstable development and test environments

Shared virtual machines change performance and exit address all the time, so API callbacks, allowlists and automated tests are hard to reproduce reliably. Remote-desktopping into a home computer can't guarantee bandwidth or stability either.

Possible outcome: test results can't be reproduced, and builds and releases stall.

OUR APPROACHOne dedicated physical Mac, one long-term static public IP on commercial fiber, and an environment that doesn't change under you.
RISK 02

Proxy payments and proxy-held accounts

Paying subscriptions through a third party, or sharing or reselling accounts, breaks most platforms' terms of service. Collecting and paying money across borders on someone else's behalf may also fall under local payment and foreign-exchange rules. And once an account registered in someone else's name is suspended, appeals and data recovery are very hard.

Possible outcome: lost accounts and history, and legal exposure for whoever paid.

OUR APPROACHWe don't top up, collect or pay on your behalf, and we don't register or hold any third-party account for you. You sign up and pay in your own name; we look after the development environment only.
RISK 03

Unclear control of data and assets

Multi-tenant VMs, and accounts or devices in someone else's name, blur who can see your business data. Moving data across borders can also bring extra compliance obligations that need assessing up front.

Possible outcome: client records, quotes and other sensitive information leak.

OUR APPROACHPhysical isolation, and the machine is yours after 15 months on service. Our staff never log in without the customer's written authorization, and data-access boundaries are stated in advance.
WE PROVIDE

What we provide

  • Setup, operation and hardware support of a dedicated macOS development environment
  • Full macOS admin rights: install any software or AI tool you like
  • A fixed public address for API callbacks and allowlist testing
  • Power, cooling and facility operations
WE DON'T PROVIDE

What we don't provide

  • Top-ups or payments on third-party platforms
  • Registering, holding, sharing or reselling third-party accounts
  • Collecting or paying money on anyone's behalf, or any settlement service
  • Network rental, bandwidth resale or anything that circumvents network controls

The above is a general description of risks and is not legal advice. Customers are responsible for making sure their use complies with local laws and with the terms of any third-party platform.

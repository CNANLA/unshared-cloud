Pricing FAQ

About pricing.

What's the difference between annual and monthly?

Annual billing pays for 12 months up front at a monthly rate 20% lower than monthly billing. Monthly is a rolling subscription you can stop anytime.

What happens after 12 months on an annual plan?

Add 3 more months on the monthly plan to reach 15 months and take the machine, or renew for another year at the annual rate.

Do prices include tax?

All prices on this site are in Canadian dollars (CA$) and exclude tax.

Do prices change if Apple changes its prices?

Signed orders keep the same price for 15 months, and if we keep operating the machine after ownership passes to you, the service fee won't go up either. If market prices fall, the lower price applies to you automatically.

Do you offer top-up or account registration services?

No. We set up and operate the development environment only. Please register and pay for third-party services in your own name.
